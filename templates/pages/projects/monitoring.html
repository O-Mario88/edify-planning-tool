Read only for everyone but the Project Coordinator. The coordinator is in control of every project activity: the two controls they hold on Project Planning (Schedule, Assign to partner) and the two decisions on partner work (Withdraw / Reassign, Resolve a hand-back — owner, 2026-09-24: "Schools assigned to project cannot be withdrawn by the staff but the project coordinator can withdraw from the partner they assigned to and reassign to another partner"). Each opens its own drawer and is checked again there and by the service behind it. Nobody else is offered a control: a reader who wants work done asks the coordinator, and the page says so rather than offering a button that would answer 403. What each reader sees is decided by apps.projects.monitoring: Impact Assessment, the Country Director and Admin read a project whole; the coordinator reads the projects they run; a CCEO or Programme Lead reads the schools they added themselves, never another officer's — one table per project they added a school to. The SSA verdict is the impact engine's (apps.projects.ssa_impact) — a missing follow-up says so and is never shown as "no change". {% endcomment %}
