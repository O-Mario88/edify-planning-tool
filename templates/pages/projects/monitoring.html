Read only for everyone but the Project Coordinator. The coordinator is in control of every project activity and gets the two controls they hold on Project Planning, Schedule and Assign to partner, which open the same drawers and are checked there. Nobody else is offered a control: a reader who wants work done asks the coordinator, and the page says so rather than offering a button that would answer 403. What each reader sees is decided by apps.projects.monitoring: Impact Assessment, the Country Director and Admin read a project whole; the coordinator reads the projects they run; a Programme Lead reads the schools they added themselves, never another officer's. The SSA verdict is the impact engine's (apps.projects.ssa_impact) — a missing follow-up says so and is never shown as "no change". {% endcomment %}
