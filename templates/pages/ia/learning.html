How these figures are madePairs, exposure, comparison, correction and grades
  • Pairs: a confirmed SSA in FY{{ view_data.prev_fy }} and FY{{ view_data.fy }} for the same school, at least 120 days apart ({{ coverage.schools_paired }} of {{ coverage.schools_in_scope }} schools in scope{% if coverage.pairs_too_close %}; {{ coverage.pairs_too_close }} assessed too close together are not compared{% endif %}).
  • Exposure: IA-verified work, a disbursed loan or a confirmed deployment dated between the school's two readings.
  • Comparison: schools that started below 7.0 on the outcome domain, with no exposure of the same kind on it. Groups under 8 schools on either side say "insufficient data".
  • Each table is one family of tests: p-values are Holm-corrected and the verdict reads the corrected value.
  • Evidence grades: insufficient (under 8 schools), descriptive (before and after, or a weakened comparison) and association (a sound comparison). No grade is causal.
