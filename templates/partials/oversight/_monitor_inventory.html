About these figures

A Core school is fully planned with two staff and two Partner visits and two staff and two Partner trainings; a Client, Core Trained or Core Graduate school with its one visit (staff's, or in a Partner's hands) and its one training. A Champion school takes donor and story visits only, so it has nothing left to plan and is counted for its project. Closed schools and deleted records are not operating and are not counted. The page checks these figures against the schools in scope before it shows them.
