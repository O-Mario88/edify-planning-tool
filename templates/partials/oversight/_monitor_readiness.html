About these figures

What counts as a staff visit is every Follow up, In-school Training and SSA Support visit staff schedule themselves; SSA Support assigned to a Partner does not count. The target is {{ pl_visits_target }} visits a year for a Programme Lead and {{ default_visits_target }} for a CCEO, whatever they hold: two at each Core school and the rest at Client, Core Trained and Core Graduate schools. Once those are planned, the schools left are the Partner's. Somebody whose schools cannot take that many visits is marked “recruit N more schools”, and is sent a notice to recruit them. The Partner takes the other two visits and two trainings at each Core school and every school beyond the staff target. Schools scheduled by staff are the schools held where staff scheduled a visit themselves; Two columns flag visits planned on both sides, and each count opens its schools. Client: Staff and Partner counts the Client, Core Trained and Core Graduate schools staff scheduled a visit at that are also in a Partner's hands; such a school takes one visit, staff's or the Partner's, so it is booked twice. A Core school takes two visits by staff and two by a Partner, so both visiting is its package and is not flagged; Core: over 2 Staff or 2 Partner visits counts the Core schools with more than two staff visits or more than two Partner visits, dated or awaiting the Partner's date. Schools in training are the schools attached to an in-school training or a group training, of the schools that take one; a cluster meeting is not a training. Core partner trainings are the Partner's two at each Core school. A Partner visit is planned once the Partner has dated it. Schools added to projects are the schools the person added to a project, of the ceilings the Project Coordinator set them, put together; what is left is what remains to reach those ceilings, and somebody with no ceiling is asked nothing. Each percentage stops at 100, so planning past one target does not cover another, and a part that asks nothing is complete. Readiness is the average of staff visits, schools in training, schools with a Partner and schools added to projects. The target warns and never refuses, and no school is assigned to a Partner by this page: the Partner's share is a target.
