About these figures

A Programme Lead plans {{ pl_visits_target }} visits a year and a CCEO {{ default_visits_target }}: two at each Core school, the rest at Client, Core Trained and Core Graduate schools. Visits count the Follow up, In-school Training and SSA Support visits the person scheduled themselves, wherever the school is; SSA Support assigned to a Partner, and donor, story, social and invitation visits, are not counted. Partner schools are the client schools beyond what the person's own visits can reach — their client, Core Trained and Core Graduate schools less their client visits — showing how many are with a partner of how many should be. Core partner visits and trainings are the partner half of each Core package, two of each a school, counting hand-overs a partner has not dated yet; Core staff trainings are the staff half's two. The partner's target is that half plus the client schools beyond staff reach. The {{ pl_visits_target }} and {{ default_visits_target }} are ceilings that warn and never refuse: a visit planned past one is saved, and the row says how many are over. Partner activities are those the partner itself has dated, with the work still awaiting the partner's date beside them. A person's visit count opens their planned visits, and their partner schools open on Partner Monitoring. Training counts a school attached to a group training or an in-school training (a cluster meeting is not a training), out of the Core, Client, Core Trained and Core Graduate schools. A school handed to a Partner is not counted under No visit or Neither, whether or not the Partner has dated it: Schools visited shows how many are with a Partner beside those with a visit planned. These are the figures Country Planning Oversight shows: the two pages count by one rulebook.
