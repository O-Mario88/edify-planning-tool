About these figures

A Programme Lead plans {{ pl_visits_target }} visits a year and a CCEO {{ default_visits_target }}: two at each Core school, the rest at Client, Core Trained and Core Graduate schools. Visits count the Follow up, In-school Training and SSA Support visits the person planned and dated, wherever the school is; donor, story, social and invitation visits are not counted. Partner schools are the schools each person handed to a partner; partner activities are those the partner itself has dated, with the work still awaiting the partner's date beside them. Training counts a school invited to a group training or cluster meeting, or given an in-school training. These are the figures Country Planning Oversight shows: the two pages count by one rulebook.
