About these figures

A Programme Lead plans {{ pl_visits_target }} visits a year and a CCEO {{ default_visits_target }}: two at each Core school, the rest at Client, Core Trained and Core Graduate schools. Visits count the Follow up and In-school Training visits the person planned and dated, wherever the school is; SSA Support (data collection), donor, story, social and invitation visits are not counted. Partner schools are the client schools beyond what the person's own visits can reach — their client, Core Trained and Core Graduate schools less their client visits — showing how many are with a partner of how many should be. Core partner visits and trainings are the partner half of each Core package, two of each a school, counting hand-overs a partner has not dated yet. Partner activities are those the partner itself has dated, with the work still awaiting the partner's date beside them. A person's visit count opens their planned visits, and their partner schools open on Partner Monitoring. Training counts a school invited to a group training or cluster meeting, or given an in-school training, out of the Core, Client, Core Trained and Core Graduate schools. These are the figures Country Planning Oversight shows: the two pages count by one rulebook.
