About these figures

Each person's own staff work in the year, as My Plan lists it. Visits completed are the visits counted toward a Programme Lead's 280 and a CCEO's 560: Follow up, In-school Training and the SSA Support staff schedule themselves. An in-school training is one visit and one training. SSA Support assigned to a Partner, and donor, story, invitation and social visits, are delivered work like any other, and are not among the visits. Completed: submitted with its visit or training ID, or approved; work started and not yet submitted is not. Counted: only work the Programme Lead has approved is counted in Visits Completed, Remaining to Visit, Schools Trained, Remaining to Train, Delivered of due, Trainings and Meetings, whatever day it was planned for; a Programme Lead's own work is approved when it is handed in. Remaining to Visit: the 280 or 560 less the visits completed and approved. Schools Trained: the schools the person holds that take a training and have had one completed and approved, an in-school training at the school or a group training it attended; training is read against the whole portfolio, never the 280 or 560. Remaining to Train: those schools not yet trained. Awaiting PL Approval: completed work waiting on the Programme Lead's approval, not counted until it has it; the Programme Lead is alerted. PL Approved: the work the Programme Lead has approved. Delivered of due: the work delivered and approved, whatever day it was planned for, against the work whose planned day has arrived or that is already delivered. Complete: delivered with the Salesforce ID and the form both in. Past date, not delivered: the planned day has passed and the work is not delivered (work delivered and waiting on a review is not counted here; the Country Director's Execution tab counts it as overdue). Partner activities are the dated Partner work each person handed over or monitors, or that is at a school they hold.
