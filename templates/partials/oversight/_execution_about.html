About these figures

Each person's own staff work in the year, as My Plan lists it. Delivered: submitted, awaiting verification or verified. Complete: delivered with the Salesforce ID and the form both in. Overdue: the planned day has passed and it is not delivered. Partner activities are the partner-delivered work each person monitors.
