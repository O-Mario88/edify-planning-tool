About these figures

Each person's own staff work in the year, as My Plan lists it. Visits completed are the visits counted toward a Programme Lead's 280 and a CCEO's 560: Follow up, In-school Training and the SSA Support staff schedule themselves. An in-school training is one visit and one training. SSA Support assigned to a Partner, and donor, story, invitation and social visits, are delivered work like any other, and are not among the visits. Completed: submitted with its visit or training ID, or verified; work started and not yet submitted is not. Remaining to Visit: the 280 or 560 less the visits completed. Schools Trained: the schools the person holds that take a training and have had one completed, an in-school training at the school or a group training it attended; training is read against the whole portfolio, never the 280 or 560. Remaining to Train: those schools not yet trained. Awaiting PL: completed work waiting on the Programme Lead, who verifies every CCEO's and Programme Lead's work. Delivered of due: the work delivered, whatever day it was planned for, against the work whose planned day has arrived or that is already delivered. Complete: delivered with the Salesforce ID and the form both in. Past date, not delivered: the planned day has passed and the work is not delivered (work delivered and waiting on a review is not counted here; the Country Director's Execution tab counts it as overdue). Partner activities are the dated Partner work each person handed over or monitors, or that is at a school they hold.
