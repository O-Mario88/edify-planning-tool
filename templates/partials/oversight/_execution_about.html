About these figures

Each person's own staff work in the year, as My Plan lists it. Visits delivered are the visits counted toward a Programme Lead's 280 and a CCEO's 560: Follow up, In-school Training and the SSA Support staff schedule themselves. An in-school training is one visit and one training. SSA Support assigned to a Partner, and donor, story, invitation and social visits, are delivered work like any other, and are not among the visits. Delivered: submitted, awaiting verification or verified. Complete: delivered with the Salesforce ID and the form both in. Past date, not delivered: the planned day has passed and the work is not delivered (work delivered and waiting on a review is not counted here; the Country Director's Execution tab counts it as overdue). Partner activities are the dated Partner work each person handed over or monitors, or that is at a school they hold.
