Nothing folds (owner, 2026-09-29: "who is online table does not collapse by PL. A record of time, not only a sitting (owner, 2026-09-28): "how long the user spend on the planning tool, which part of the tool they accessed and for how long, working on what, how many times they logged in, when they logged in", for a day, week, month, quarter or FY.
