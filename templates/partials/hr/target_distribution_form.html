{% load frontend_filters %} {% comment %} The IA → Program Lead distribution drawer, as an envelope. It used to render every eligible lead pre-filled with a recommendation and a server-rendered balance that went stale the moment a number was edited. The person distributing thinks the other way round: pick a lead, give them a number, watch what is left fall to zero. So the drawer starts with only the leads who already hold an allocation; everyone else waits in one dropdown, and the remaining figure recomputes on every keystroke (static/js/ target-distribution.js). The engine still reconciles server-side on save and approve — the live figure is the working preview, not the law. Every eligible lead's row exists in the DOM from the start, hidden until picked. Hidden inputs still submit, and an empty target is the deletion signal `_save_allocation_rows` already honours — so returning a lead to the dropdown and saving removes their draft without any extra wiring. {% endcomment %}
