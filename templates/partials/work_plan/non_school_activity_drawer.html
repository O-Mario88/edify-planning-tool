The exception is thrown before the request is sent, so the cost preview never fired at all — the panel sat on "Enter dates and participants to preview the catalogue cost" no matter what was entered, and the planner scheduled non-school work without ever seeing what the CD catalogue would charge for it. Overriding the inherited value per control is what makes the preview reachable. {% endcomment %}
