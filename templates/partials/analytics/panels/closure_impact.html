{% comment %} The School Closures tab — the country picture of closure. Separate from Closure Quality on purpose. That tab is a worklist of records to distrust; this one is country performance, and mixing work-to-fix with what-we-lost leaves nobody sure which numbers they are accountable for. The concentration question drives the layout: eight schools lost across eight districts is country-wide drift, eight lost in one district is a district in trouble, and a national total cannot tell them apart. The scope caption stays with the panel rather than the workspace header. The header names the workspace; this line names whose ground these numbers describe, because an RVP reading "the country lost 40 schools" would reasonably assume the country when the figure covers their regions. {% endcomment %}
