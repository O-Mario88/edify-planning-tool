Measurement framework

Connect training, lending and educational technology to an intended school outcome before interpreting delivery as success.

  1. Choose the SSA intervention and explain the programme's expected contribution.
  2. Define baseline eligibility, expected direction, meaningful change and the follow-up window.
  3. Publish the measurement mapping through the existing approval workflow; retain its version for enrolled schools.
  4. Review domain scores with classroom observations, discipleship evidence and school feedback. Record limitations in the impact report.
