Balance is never negative: schools above the ceiling are Excess. Status flags each row: above its ceiling (more schools than it should have), below it (fewer than it should have) or at it. Every figure opens the schools it counts, and the lead sets or changes a ceiling from the row. Figures: apps.planning.training_ceilings.team_summary and .summary_sections, counted from the plans themselves. Its shape is Project Capacity's (owner, 2026-10-06: "use project capacity table format, but more compact, and each lead should have their own"): one tab an officer, as that page gives one tab a project; under it the officer's name line with their figures added up, then a compact table whose rows are their trainings — Ceiling for Maximum, Total Scheduled for Assigned, Balance for Remaining, the same Utilization and Status, and the row's Actions menu. The strip is `tabState` (alpine-components.js), so the officer chosen survives a paginated link and the address bar; every panel is in the page, the chosen one painted first, because a lead's team is a handful of officers with a handful of trainings each. {% endcomment %}
