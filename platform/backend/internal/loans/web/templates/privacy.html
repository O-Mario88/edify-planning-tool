What we collect

The school's name, location and enrollment; the director's name, phone number and (if given) email; the school's average fees actually collected per term; the loan purpose, amount, preferred repayment period and the lending partner chosen; and, only if you choose to answer, the director's religion.

Why we use it

To pass your loan request to the lending partner you chose, follow it up with them, and record its outcome; and to report, in total, on the loans schools receive through Edify. Edify does not make the lending decision and does not use this information to score the application.

Who receives it

Edify staff responsible for school finance and loan follow-up, and the one lending partner you chose. It is never sent to any other lender. The director's religion is never sent to the lender and plays no part in any decision.

How long we keep it

For as long as Edify needs it to follow up the referral and to keep its records, under Edify's data-retention policy. You may ask Edify to correct it, or to stop using it where the law allows.
